What this does

  1. Detection — classifies the leaf as Healthy or one of 3 WCLWD symptom types, with an out-of-distribution check.
  2. Explainability (WSSS) — Grad-CAM + GrabCut produce a pixel-level pseudo-segmentation mask of the diseased region, with no manual annotation.
  3. ABCD Rule + Severity — Asymmetry, Border irregularity, Colour variation and Distribution are combined with affected area into a Disease Severity Index (0-100), mapped to Mild / Moderate / Severe / Critical.
